How the contest works

  1. Play any game. Quizzes, an arcade shooter, a wheel puzzle, and more.
  2. Hit the target score. Each game unlocks its own secret code word.
  3. Climb the leaderboard. Codes you unlock are added for you. Most codes by the end of the week wins.
